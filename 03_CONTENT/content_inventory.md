Good observation.

Most designers make the mistake of designing around **"Who we are"**.

But businesses like Agila do not make money when people read about them.

They make money when people:

1. Contact them.
2. Request a quote.
3. Ask for staff.
4. Submit hiring requirements.

Therefore, the website should be designed around a conversion funnel:

```text
VISITOR
   ↓
Trust
   ↓
Understand Service
   ↓
See Credibility
   ↓
Contact Agila
```

Not:

```text
VISITOR
   ↓
Read About Us
   ↓
Read Services
   ↓
Leave
```

---

# New Priority Hierarchy

## Priority 1

CONTACT US

This is the primary goal.

Everything should eventually push toward:

```text
Request Staff
Get a Quote
Contact Us
```

---

## Priority 2

COMPANY PORTFOLIO

Not "About Us"

Portfolio.

Because businesses want proof.

They want answers to:

```text
Who have you worked with?
What industries?
What projects?
What workforce scale?
```

The current website barely shows this.

The redesign should elevate it.

---

## Priority 3

Services

Staffing.
Direct Recruitment.
Hire-to-Permanent.

---

## Priority 4

Company Story

Who Agila is.

This should come later.

# What This Means For The Prototype

The navigation should be reordered:

```text
Home

Request Staff

Portfolio

Industries

Services

About

Contact
```

Notice:

**Portfolio** and **Contact** move up.

**About Us** moves down.

Because visitors don't come to Agila to learn history.

They come to solve a staffing problem.

The entire prototype should subtly guide users toward contacting Agila while using the portfolio and industry expertise to build enough trust for them to take that action.
